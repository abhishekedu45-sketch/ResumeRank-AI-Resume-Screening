Privacy Policy

ResumeRank — BCA Final-Year Academic Project
Last updated: 5 November 2026

Welcome to ResumeRank, an AI-assisted Resume Screening and Ranking System developed as a BCA final-year academic project. This Privacy Policy explains what information candidates submit, how the project uses that information and how candidates can contact the project administrator with questions or requests.

1. Information We Collect

When a candidate submits an application through this project, the following information may be collected:

  • Full name
  • Address
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Uploaded resume in PDF format
  • Professional information contained in the resume

2. How We Use Your Information

Submitted information is used for the purposes of demonstrating and operating the ResumeRank academic project, including:

  • Receiving and managing candidate applications.
  • Analysing resume content using AI-assisted techniques.
  • Comparing candidate skills with job requirements.
  • Generating candidate match scores and rankings.
  • Sending application-related or interview-related emails.

3. AI-Based Resume Screening

ResumeRank compares information extracted from submitted resumes with the job description and required skills entered by the administrator. The system generates match scores to assist candidate evaluation. These scores are intended as supporting information and should not be treated as the sole basis for recruitment or hiring decisions.

4. Access to and Sharing of Information

Candidate information may be viewed by the authorised project administrator. Technical service providers, including hosting and email providers, may process relevant information when necessary to operate the application and its features.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Candidate details, screening results and uploaded resumes may be stored using the application's database and file storage facilities. Information should be retained only for the period required for the stated project or recruitment purposes and applicable legal requirements. Candidates may contact the administrator to ask about deletion of their submitted information.

6. Data Protection

Reasonable technical measures are used to help protect information submitted through the application. However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security. Candidates should avoid submitting information that is not necessary for their application.

7. Candidate Requests

Candidates may contact the project administrator to ask questions about their submitted information or request correction or deletion. Requests will be considered subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate project or recruitment needs.

8. Third-Party Services

ResumeRank may use third-party hosting, email and technical services to provide its features. These providers may process information as necessary to operate those services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.
